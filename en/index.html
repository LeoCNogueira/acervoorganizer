Frequently asked questions

Is Acervo Organizer free?

The download is free and the first two weeks come with everything unlocked. After that, analysis, simulation, duplicates, integrity, comparison, reports and undo stay free. To keep organizing, importing and moving files, the unlock is a one-time purchase, no subscription.

Can the app delete or damage my files?

No. It never deletes and never overwrites. Duplicates and problem files are moved to their own folders, and every organization can be undone. Between different drives, the app copies, verifies, and only then removes the original.

Does it work with a NAS?

Yes, with the NAS volume mounted on your Mac, such as a Synology or QNAP over the network. The app checks the connection during long operations and stops safely if it drops; what was already moved is recorded and can be undone.

Does it work on my Mac?

It works on Macs with macOS 13 or later, with Apple silicon or Intel processors.

What about my Lightroom catalog?

XMP files travel with the photos. If the folders are already in a Lightroom Classic catalog, moving photos outside Lightroom makes the catalog lose track of them; after organizing, use Lightroom's option to find the missing folder and point it to the new place.

Which formats are supported?

Photos in JPG, HEIC, PNG, TIFF and the main RAW formats (CR3, CR2, NEF, ARW, RAF, DNG, ORF, RW2 and others), with XMP. Videos in MOV, MP4, MTS, MKV, AVI and others, with .SRT, .LRF, .LRV and .THM. 360° videos from DJI (.OSV) and Insta360 (.insv and .insp).

Does the app collect my data?

No. There is no account, no ads, no usage analytics and no tracking, and your photos never leave your drives. See the privacy policy.

Can I use it on more than one Mac?

Yes. The unlock works on Macs signed in to the same App Store account. On a new Mac, go to Settings, License, View Options…, Restore Purchases.

Which languages does the app support?

English and Portuguese (Brazil). The app follows your macOS language; to choose another one just for Acervo Organizer, go to System Settings › General › Language & Region › Applications. New users get destination folders named PHOTOS, VIDEOS, DUPLICATES and PROBLEMS.
