Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Acervo Organizer is a Mac app that organizes photos and videos in the folders you choose. It is designed to work on your computer without sending your work anywhere.

Data we collect

None. Acervo Organizer has no accounts, shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking, and sends the developer no information about you, your photos or how you use the app.

Where your files are

The app only accesses the folders you choose. Photos and videos are analyzed and moved only on your Mac and on the drives you choose (for example an external drive or a NAS). To work, the app stores on your Mac your preferences, access permission for the folders you chose, the history of organizations (used for undo) and an optional analysis cache, which can be cleared in Settings. None of this leaves your computer.

Purchases

The free trial and the unlock are processed by Apple through the App Store, under the Apple Privacy Policy. The developer receives no payment details or personal information. The app only asks the App Store whether the trial or the unlock is active.

Images of people

Photos of identifiable people may be personal data under data protection laws such as Brazil’s LGPD and the GDPR. Because Acervo Organizer does not send or store your images outside your own drives, you, the photographer, stay in control of that data.
