Support

Need help or found a problem? Email support@internetmidia.com with your macOS version, the app version (at the bottom of the sidebar) and, if you use a NAS, its model. For folder access problems, include the report from Settings › Folder access diagnostics › Copy Report.

Frequently asked questions

Does Acervo Organizer delete or overwrite files?
No. If a file with the same name already exists at the destination, it is kept and the new one gets a (2), (3)… suffix or stays where it is. Duplicates and problem files are moved to the DUPLICATES and PROBLEMS folders, never to the Trash. (If you used the app before version 2.10, your folders keep their original names: DUPLICADOS and COM PROBLEMAS.)

How do I undo an organization?
Each organize screen shows its history. Click Undo… to move every file back to its original place. Undo is never locked, even after the trial ends.

Can I see what will happen before moving anything?
Yes. Use Simulate Move: the app shows each file’s destination and any name conflicts without moving anything.

My NAS disconnected while organizing.
The app stops safely. Everything already moved is recorded and can be undone. Reconnect the NAS and analyze the folder again to continue.

Are my drone’s .SRT and .LRF files separated from the videos?
No. Telemetry (.SRT), proxies (.LRF/.LRV) and thumbnails (.THM) stay with each video. In Settings › Video organization, choose whether proxies go with the video, to a separate folder (_PROXIES), or stay where they are.

How does Import Card work?
The app copies the card to folders by date and session, checks every copy by checksum (SHA-256) and can make a second copy on another drive. The card is only read, files already imported are skipped, and nothing at the destination is overwritten. When everything is verified, the app can eject the card.

The app asks for my folder again when it opens.
If the folder is on an external drive or a disconnected NAS, connect it and choose the folder again. The app will remember it.

How do I use the app in English or in Portuguese?
The app follows your macOS language. To choose a language just for Acervo Organizer, go to System Settings › General › Language & Region › Applications, add Acervo Organizer and pick a language, then reopen the app.

How do the free trial and the unlock work?
Every feature is available for 14 days. Afterwards, analysis, simulation, duplicates, integrity checks, folder comparison, reports and undo remain free. To keep organizing, importing and moving files, the unlock is a one-time purchase, no subscription. New Mac or reinstalled? Use Settings › License › View Options… › Restore Purchases.
